Discord Integration

Owner Change Announcer

Planet owner changes detected during a full galaxy scan are posted to this channel. Leave empty to disable. Use !getid in a channel to get its ID.

Population Drop Announcer

Planet population drops (same owner, population fell — attacks/sieges) are posted to this channel, separate from owner changes. Leave empty to disable. Use !getid in a channel to get its ID.

Battle Report Announcer

Battle reports now sync from the game API globally (every battle on the server, not just your own alliance's) so nothing is missed while a wider view is decided on later. This channel only gets a post when a battle involves one of the alliance tags below — leave the tag list empty and nothing will announce, even with a channel set. Use !getid in a channel to get its ID.

New Player Announcer

New players (join date synced from the game API) are posted to this channel by the kombat bot (same identity as the Battle Challenge Tracker). Leave empty to disable — new players still sync and will announce once a channel is set. Use !getid in a channel to get its ID.

Battle Challenge Tracker

Twice-daily CV/population leaderboard posts to this channel. CV/pop ratios control how raw kills convert to points; excluded alliance tags (comma-separated) are left out of both leaderboards entirely, in addition to always-excluded friendly fire. Leave the channel empty to disable posting.

Incoming Attack Alerts

Incoming-attack notifications (from the in-game forwarder webhook) are posted here, with the list of allied fleets that can defend in time. Leave empty to disable.

Command Blocklist (Public Channels)

In these channels the bot ignores commands and replies with harmless garbled static instead — use it for public/guest channels. Enter channel IDs separated by commas or new lines. Run !getid in a channel before blocking it to grab its ID.

Removes all fleet signatures that haven't been updated in over 10 days to maintain dashboard speed.

Nuke All Intel - New Round

Obliterates ALL systems, planets, fleets, and map data. User accounts remain. Requires Master Admin password.

Player names, ids and system names are archived first, in the same transaction — if the snapshot fails, nothing is deleted.

Round Archive

Who was who, per round. A player id is stable for the life of an account but the name is not, so this is what lets the intel panel say that Chewie played last round as Elfenlied. Snapshot while the round is still live to catch players who go inactive before it ends.
